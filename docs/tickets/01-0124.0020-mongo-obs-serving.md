# Mongo obs source station serving

- **Status:** Planned
- **Type:** AFK
- **Depends on:** [scatter substrate](./done/01-0124.0010-scatter-substrate.md) (`ScatterDomain`,
  origin identity and `Capability.origins`, run-free origins, the loosened binder)
- **Blocks:** [Mongo forecast-run archive source](./01-0134-forecast-run-archive-source.md) (shares
  the transport and registry), [obs+forecast reach composition](./01-0137-obs-forecast-reach-composition.md)
  (a real scatter reach to compose).
- **Outcome:** Hourly station observations from the operator's Collector database served through the
  projection algebra as a read-only private source in a composition of its own, with per-parameter
  provenance naming the network, method, and station.

## Parent

Second slice of the [Mongo obs source](./01-0124-mongo-obs-source.md) — the source itself, riding
the [first slice](./done/01-0124.0010-scatter-substrate.md)'s contracts. The parent holds the align
resolutions (2026-08-21) and the union of criteria.

## What to build

The parent's resolved source, end to end: the held `AsyncMongoClient` behind `Closeable`
(constructed sync in `compose()`, released through `Gateway.aclose()`), the read-only guard, the
`METEOSCAPE_COLLECTOR_OBS_URI` secret, the sibling timeline wrapper over the reused
`TimelineProbe`/`TapTable` machinery, the Mongo probe decoding both collector schemas through one
canonical mapping, the registry read with serving-path re-read past the configured interval, and
`collector-obs:stations` with origin identity per
[ADR-0003](../adr/0003-provenance-and-origin.md): declared ahead of request through
`Capability.origins` (from the registry snapshot), stamped at serve with authority and process
read off the documents themselves and unit — the station's name or network id — from the
addressing record; declaration and stamp normally equal, divergence visible.

Composed **standalone**: an obs-only profile tested through `Gateway`; no pilot composition root,
no shared parameter beside a forecast producer
(→ [obs+forecast reach composition](./01-0137-obs-forecast-reach-composition.md)). Observed wind is
declared as measured `wind_speed`/`wind_direction`, not decoded to u/v — honest to the instrument,
and it keeps the scatter reach out of the wind calculator's input fold until the fold work lands.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A request over an archived window at a registered station serves hourly observation series
      for the mapped canonical parameters; provenance names `collector-obs:stations` and carries
      origin identity (authority = the network, process = the method, unit = the station's
      name / network id — never the coordinate-derived registry key).
- [ ] An unregistered location or a window outside the declared estimate declines at admission; an
      admitted ask with an empty overlap fails as `capability-mismatch` at serve — never served
      empty or padded. Holes inside a served overlap are nodata.
- [ ] Both collector schemas serve through one canonical mapping; parameters the collector does not
      write for a station's source type are absent from that capability, not null-filled.
- [ ] The collector schema contract is pinned by integration fixtures sampled from the live
      database; a schema change fails the suite, never silently misreads at runtime.
- [ ] The source is read-only by construction, pinned by a guard — no write/update/insert reaches
      the Mongo client.
- [ ] `Capability.origins` for an observation parameter pairs each station's coordinates with its
      declared origin — the request-forming discovery, populated from the registry and equal to
      what a subsequent serve stamps.
- [ ] A station added to the collector's registry becomes servable without a process restart,
      pinned by advancing a fake clock past the re-read interval — not by restarting the fixture.
- [ ] Releasing the composition through `Gateway.aclose()` closes the held client — the 0116 seam's
      first real holder, pinned by a connection-liveness check, not a mock call count.
