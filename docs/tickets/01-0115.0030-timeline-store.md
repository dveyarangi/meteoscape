## What to build

~350–420 raw lines (002/003b-scale; the largest slice, kept whole because a half-store serves
nothing):

- **`quantize`** — `ground`'s store-side sibling (read the request-side verb first): a per-axis
  fold delegating to `Axis.clip`; latticed X/Y resolve to pinned containing cells, unit-deferred
  axes (T, Z) take `ANY`, anything else identity → parent, *quantize is ground's store-side
  sibling* (including the #22/#23 deferrals and their escape hatch).
- The **retentive in-memory `Store`**: unit-granular (`(parameter, per-axis cells, window)`),
  private lattices (spatial from `StoreSpec`; T inherited from answers), `capability` = what it
  holds, the per-unit **{held, fresh, origin}** report, **covers-or-refetch-whole** semantics,
  `assimilate` consuming the answer and slicing per parameter inside the store → parent, *Serve
  gate* and *`assimilate` consumes the answer*.
- **Retention interval** honored (housekeeping only) and the retentive `StoreFactory` honoring the
  configured `StoreSpec`.
- Refill *ask construction*: the store shape as a `SelectionDomain` — pinned X/Y cells plus
  boundless T/Z, never enumerable → parent, *Refill authors a `SelectionDomain`*.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given the timeline unit definition, `quantize` produces boundless T/Z and pinned containing
      X/Y cells; given an inverted (grid-style) unit definition, the same fold produces the
      inverted ask — no axis role is hardcoded.
- [ ] `assimilate` accepts a multi-domain answer and the store afterwards reports units keyed by
      the answer's native cells; a guard test fails if any component outside the store constructs a
      unit `Selection` or reads the lattice.
- [ ] Freshness is honored per unit: an unexpired unit reports servable, an expired one reports
      refetch-needed (differing expirations mocked — a single-cadence source's units age together).
- [ ] A quantized request a retained window fully covers is reported servable; any lesser overlap
      (extension, disjoint, regrow) reports whole-unit refetch; after re-assimilation exactly one
      window per unit exists, single-origin — a spliced `valid_time` is unrepresentable.
- [ ] Units older than the retention interval are evicted; eviction never widens what is reported
      servable.
- [ ] All of the above is demonstrated by store-level tests with no `Reservoir` wiring.
